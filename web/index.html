Accounting grid

A ledger of a million journal lines as a pivot table, written in F# and compiled to WebAssembly GC. Rows and columns fold to their totals, the table turns (accounts, cost centres, periods), and the lines behind a cell open as a tab. The whole interface is drawn by the module onto one canvas; JavaScript only forwards pointer, key and size events (lib/runtime/runtime/gfx.js).
